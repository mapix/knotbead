About the Game

Untangle taut woven cords across 50 handcrafted 3D spatial puzzles. Drag cord ends to open sockets or drop onto another cord end to swap both in one smooth gesture. Every freed cord automatically threads itself with luminous ceramic, nacre pearl, and faceted crystal beads on your linen workbench.

  • 50 Handcrafted Spatial Levels: Multi-stage topological cord puzzles spanning 5 to 9 interwoven strands.
  • Chain Combo Beadwork: Free cords on consecutive moves to light up Pearl and Faceted Crystal bead tiers and earn 3 stars.
  • 100% Offline & Privacy-First: No ads, no tracking, no third-party analytics.

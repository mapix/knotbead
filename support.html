Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How do I swap two cord ends?
A: Drag any cord end directly onto another occupied socket and release to swap both ends in a single move.

Q: How do I earn 3 stars and brighter beads?
A: Free cords on consecutive moves to build a Chain combo, upgrading strands from Plain Beads to Nacre Pearl and Faceted Crystal beads.
